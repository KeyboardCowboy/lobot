An entry goes in once a real choice between options has been made and someone with authority has approved it, or still has to. Statements of focus, PM tasks, inherited project facts and technical architecture don't belong here.

  1. Two or more options were on the table and one was picked.
  2. Someone pushed for it, and someone signed off or still has to.
  3. It changes what gets built, how it behaves, the design, the scope or a commitment.

Spotted one? Use Propose a decision below. It's filed as pending until the PM confirms who approved it.

Example of the shape: "Don't use CSS hyphenation", not "Hyphenation".
